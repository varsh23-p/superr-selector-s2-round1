Out-of-Sample Split Validation

Players were rated on odd-numbered matches and tested on even-numbered matches to measure true predictive signal:

  • Batters Rank Correlation: 0.15 vs 0.05 (3× baseline)
  • Bowlers Signal: Softer / higher variance
  • Threshold Sensitivity: 5–10 of 10 names retained

Separate Shrinkage & Opponent Quality

Expected runs and dismissal probabilities are conditioned on over, wickets, required rate, pace/spin, PI, and competition (IPL vs T20I) without player names.

Crucially, runs and wickets are shrunk separately toward the average because run scoring stabilizes far faster than wicket events—preventing small-sample wicket flukes from distorting rankings.

Data Cleaning & Known Caveats

  • Extras Code Reconstruction: Extras columns store event codes (1=NB, 2=WD, 3=LB, 4=Bye). Rebuilt totals match 405 "won by N runs" margins within 2 runs ~58% of the time (unrecorded bye/leg-bye counts explain the gap).
  • Future Roadmap: Incorporate pitch conditions, dew, venue factors, and team strength as multi-season sample sizes grow.
